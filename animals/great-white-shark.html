Great White Shark

Predator

Great white sharks are among the ocean’s most powerful predators. They use strong bodies, sharp senses, and quick bursts of speed to stalk and catch prey in open water.

These sharks often patrol coastal waters and deep offshore regions. They are known for their ability to detect even tiny traces of blood and movement in the water around them.

  • Habitat: Temperate coastal oceans
  • Diet: Fish, rays, and seals
  • Size: Up to 20 feet long
  • Fun fact: They can detect blood from far away.
