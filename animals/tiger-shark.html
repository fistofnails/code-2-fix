Tiger Shark

Predator

Tiger sharks are powerful swimmers with a distinctive striped pattern when young. They are known for their versatility and broad diet, making them efficient ocean hunters.

They can be found in warm coastal and open water habitats and often explore many different areas in search of food. Their strong jaws and adaptable hunting style make them remarkable marine predators.

  • Habitat: Tropical and subtropical waters
  • Diet: Fish, crustaceans, and birds
  • Pattern: Stripes fade with age
  • Fun fact: They are one of the most diverse feeders in the ocean.
