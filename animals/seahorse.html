Seahorse

Fish

Seahorses are tiny, unusual fish that wrap their tails around seagrass and coral. They move slowly and blend into underwater plants with remarkable precision.

These animals are famous for their parenting behavior, because the male carries the eggs in a brood pouch until they hatch. Their delicate appearance hides their strong survival instincts.

  • Habitat: Seagrass beds
  • Diet: Tiny crustaceans
  • Movement: Slow and graceful
  • Fun fact: Males carry the eggs until hatching.
