Angelfish

Fish

Angelfish are graceful reef dwellers with elegant fins and brilliant coloration. They glide over coral heads and reef edges with calm, controlled movement.

They feed on algae, sponge, and tiny bits of coral life. Their bright patterns and behavior make them one of the most eye-catching fish found near tropical reefs.

  • Habitat: Reefs and lagoons
  • Diet: Algae and sponges
  • Appearance: Bright fins and stripes
  • Fun fact: Some species form strong territory bonds.
