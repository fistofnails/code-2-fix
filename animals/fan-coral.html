Fan Coral

Coral

Fan coral grows in broad, layered branches that spread out like a hand or fan. Its shape helps it catch drifting food carried by ocean currents.

Coral reefs depend on animals like fan coral to provide structure and shelter. These branching forms create safe spaces for fish, invertebrates, and smaller reef animals.

  • Habitat: Warm reef waters
  • Role: Shelter and food capture
  • Shape: Fan-like branches
  • Fun fact: It helps create complex reef structure.
