Brain Coral

Coral

Brain coral gets its name from the grooved pattern that resembles the folds of a human brain. It grows in large rounded masses and forms one of the classic structures of a healthy reef.

Coral is essential for reef ecosystems, because it creates shelter for countless marine species. Brain coral plays a major role in supporting biodiversity and protecting coastlines from wave energy.

  • Habitat: Tropical reefs
  • Role: Reef builder
  • Shape: Rounded and bumpy
  • Fun fact: It can grow slowly over many years.
