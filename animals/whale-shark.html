Whale Shark

Fish

The whale shark is the largest fish in the world, but it is a gentle filter feeder. Instead of hunting large prey, it swims with its huge mouth open and filters tiny animals out of the water.

These calm giants often gather in warm tropical waters where plankton is plentiful. Their spotted skin and slow movement make them one of the most fascinating animals in the ocean.

  • Habitat: Tropical oceans
  • Diet: Plankton and krill
  • Size: Up to 40 feet long
  • Fun fact: They can grow bigger than a bus.
