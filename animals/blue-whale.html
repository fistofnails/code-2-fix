Blue Whale

Mammal

The blue whale is the largest animal ever known to live on Earth. It can travel great distances across open water and is famous for its enormous size, powerful tail, and deep underwater songs.

These gentle giants feed mostly on krill, gulping huge amounts of water and filtering out their tiny prey. They live in the open ocean and often migrate long distances between feeding and breeding grounds.

  • Habitat: Open ocean
  • Diet: Krill
  • Speed: Up to around 30 mph in bursts
  • Fun fact: Their calls can travel for miles underwater.
