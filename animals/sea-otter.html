Sea Otter

Mammal

Sea otters are playful marine mammals that spend much of their time floating in kelp forests. They use rocks as tools to crack open shellfish and sea urchins.

Their thick fur helps them stay warm in cold waters, and they play a huge role in protecting kelp forests by controlling sea urchin populations.

  • Habitat: Kelp forests
  • Diet: Sea urchins, mussels, and crabs
  • Specialty: Uses tools to open food
  • Fun fact: They have the densest fur of any mammal.
