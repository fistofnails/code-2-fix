Clownfish

Fish

Clownfish are bright, lively fish that live among sea anemones. Their bold orange bodies and white stripes make them easy to spot in reef habitats.

These fish are protected by a special mucus layer, which allows them to live safely inside anemones without being stung. They help keep the reef ecosystem balanced by feeding on small organisms.

  • Habitat: Warm coral reefs
  • Diet: Small crustaceans and algae
  • Behavior: Lives in social groups
  • Fun fact: They can live inside sea anemones without being harmed.
