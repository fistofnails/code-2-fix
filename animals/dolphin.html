Dolphin

Mammal

Dolphins are highly intelligent marine mammals known for their playful nature and social behavior. They live in pods and communicate through clicks, whistles, and body language.

Their speed, teamwork, and curiosity make them excellent hunters. Dolphins often work together to herd fish and protect each other while swimming in open water.

  • Habitat: Coastal and open waters
  • Diet: Fish and squid
  • Group: Pods
  • Fun fact: They can recognize themselves in mirrors.
