Jellyfish

Invertebrate

Jellyfish drift with ocean currents and use their soft, translucent bodies to move through the water. Their tentacles hold stinging cells that help them catch prey.

Some jellyfish glow in the dark, creating beautiful displays in deep water at night. Even though they seem delicate, they are highly effective swimmers and hunters.

  • Habitat: Oceans worldwide
  • Diet: Plankton and small fish
  • Movement: Drifts with currents
  • Fun fact: Some glow with bioluminescence.
