Octopus

Cephalopod

Octopuses are clever invertebrates with soft bodies and exceptional camouflage. They can change color and texture to blend into coral, rocks, and the seafloor.

Their intelligence is remarkable. Octopuses solve problems, open jars, and use their eight arms to explore tight spaces and hunt for prey.

  • Habitat: Rocky reefs and seafloor
  • Diet: Crabs and shellfish
  • Body: Eight arms and three hearts
  • Fun fact: They can squeeze through very small openings.
