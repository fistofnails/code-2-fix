Sea Turtle

Reptile

Sea turtles spend most of their lives in the ocean and use their flippers to glide through warm and tropical waters. They travel long distances between feeding grounds and nesting beaches.

These ancient animals are important to marine ecosystems because they help maintain seagrass beds and keep food webs balanced. Many species are currently under protection because their numbers are threatened.

  • Habitat: Coastal waters and open oceans
  • Diet: Seagrass, algae, and jellyfish
  • Age: Some live for many decades
  • Fun fact: They often return to the beach where they were born.
